The rules
Activate a tab → a sheen sweeps across the pill once, 800ms — light acknowledging the touch.
The card that needs you wears an orbiting gradient border (6s linear, GPU-cheap: one animated angle) — perpetual, but only while something waits.
The Deck opens on frosted glass: backdrop blur fades in over 450ms, panes dim behind it.
Pane switches cross-fade with a 1% scale instead of hard-cutting.

Tradeoff: the most striking — and the most at odds with Sentinel's flat-instrument doctrine. Blur + perpetual gradients cost GPU with 10 panes live, and the orbit border must be strictly reserved for "needs you" or it becomes noise.
Honest note: this direction bends the app's own "idle looks idle" rule — pick it only if the showpiece feel is worth that.
