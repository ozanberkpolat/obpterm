Six words, one glance

printinga tiny 3-bar meter dances beside the name — bytes are flowing right now. Stops the instant output stops.
workinga sonar ring leaves the dot every 2.4s — the agent is thinking even when the screen is quiet. A working row that stops ringing = the "quiet Nm" you should look at.
needs youthe knock: the whole row taps twice, rests ~3.5s, taps again — the rhythm of someone at your door, impossible to confuse with work.
danger aska sharp red double-blink — different color, faster pulse. Your eye learns the difference between "may I?" and "are you sure?" without reading a word.
doneone soft pop when it lands, then perfectly still blue. Done is calm — it never loops.
progressthe sliver under the row creeps forward — position is the message (already shipped, kept).

The grammar
· Idle, sleeping and read rows are perfectly still — stillness is a state too, so motion always means something changed.
· Every animation is transform/opacity only — GPU-cheap with 10 tabs live.
· Each state owns a rhythm, not just a color: sonar ≈ 2.4s, knock ≈ 4s, danger ≈ 3s — distinguishable in peripheral vision, slow enough to never feel busy.
· Projects, colors, grouping, the bottom bar: untouched.
